About Us

Lyceum of Southern Luzon Inc. is a premier institution committed to academic excellence and the holistic development of every student. Established to serve the people of Southern Luzon, we offer a vibrant learning environment with modern facilities, dedicated and experienced faculty, and a vision to shape future leaders and innovators.

🎓 Quality Education

Programs designed to meet industry standards and prepare students for real-world careers.

👩‍🏫 Dedicated Faculty

Experienced educators who guide and support students in every step of their journey.

🏫 Supportive Environment

A safe and welcoming campus where students can learn, grow, and excel.

Admission

Join the Lyceum of Southern Luzon family! Admission is open to all qualified students who aspire to excel and grow in a supportive academic environment.

Choose your department

For Junior High School (Grades 7–10) and Senior High School (Grades 11–12). Check the High School Department Facebook page for the latest enrollment announcements.

  • Completed enrollment/application form (available at the school)
  • Original Report Card (Form 138)
  • Learner's Permanent Record (Form 137) – to be requested by the school from the previous school
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • Birth certificate (PSA authenticated), photocopy
  • 2x2 ID photos
  • For Senior High School: Grade 10 completion certificate and chosen track (Academic or Technical-Vocational)

For BS Information Systems, BS Management Accounting, BS Customs Administration, and BS Criminology.

  • Completed application form (available at the school or online)
  • Certified true copy of high school diploma or transcript of records
  • Birth certificate (PSA authenticated)
  • 2x2 ID photos
  • Entrance examination or interview (schedule to be announced)

For inquiries about admission schedules and procedures, please contact our Admissions Office or message us on our Facebook page.
